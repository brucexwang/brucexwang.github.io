About Me

I am currently a Research Assistant Professor at the Department of English and Communication , The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

Research interests:

  • Forensic phonetics
  • Evaluation of voice evidence in English, Cantonese, and Mandarin
  • Articulatory phoentics (especially disordered speech/ultrasound/EMA)
  • Sociophonetics

Prospective PhD students interested in any of these research areas are welcome to contact me.
